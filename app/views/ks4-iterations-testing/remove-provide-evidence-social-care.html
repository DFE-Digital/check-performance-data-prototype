Provide evidence for the removal of Billy B

What evidence do I need?

Do:

  • provide evidence from social services, the police, or another relevant external organisation
  • include external evidence, as a statement from your school or college on its own is not enough
  • provide at least one piece of evidence dated in 2026 and before the start of exams on 7 May 2026
  • provide evidence showing social services' involvement with the pupil or their family, where applicable
  • make sure evidence from social services explains:
    • the circumstances affecting the pupil
    • how these circumstances had a severe and significant impact on the pupil's ability to access education or sit exams, either in school or online
  • provide a police report if the pupil has been involved with the police
  • use the free-text box to explain how police involvement had a severe and significant impact on the pupil's ability to access education or sit exams
  • use this category for pupils who:
    • have been the victim of a crime
    • have been involved with the police because they committed a crime

Do not:

  • submit a request without third party evidence
  • use a school or college statement as the only evidence
  • copy and paste information from multiple reports into a single document where the original source cannot be verified
  • submit documents that do not clearly show:
    • document headers
    • dates
    • that the document is complete and unchanged
  • combine documents in a way that makes it difficult to verify their authenticity

In 2025, 1,214 pupil removal requests were submitted in this category. Only 6% were approved, while 94% were rejected.
