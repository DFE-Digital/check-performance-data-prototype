Life-limiting or critical illness

Provide evidence for the removal of Billy B

What evidence do I need?

Do:

  • provide medical evidence from an external healthcare professional
  • make sure the evidence:
    • is on official headed paper
    • includes the healthcare professional's name and job title
    • is signed, either electronically or by hand
  • provide at least one piece of medical evidence dated in 2026 and before the start of exams on 7 May 2026
  • make sure the evidence explains how the illness or injury had a severe impact on the pupil's ability to access education or sit their exams, either in school or online
  • provide external medical evidence, as a school or college statement on its own is not enough

Do not:

  • submit a request without external medical evidence
  • submit extracts from medical reports that do not clearly show the impact of the illness or injury on the pupil's ability to access education or sit their exams
  • request the removal of a pupil with a short-term illness, such as a viral infection that caused them to miss up to a month of school
  • request the removal of a pupil receiving SEN support for a disability, illness or injury, unless they have also been diagnosed with a separate, unrelated illness or injury
  • request the removal of a pupil whose illness or injury is managed day to day and who is still able to access education, either in school or online
  • combine or edit documents in a way that makes them difficult to verify, for example by merging reports together or removing dates, headers or other identifying information

In 2025, 794 pupil removal requests were submitted in this category. Only 8% were approved, while 92% were rejected.
