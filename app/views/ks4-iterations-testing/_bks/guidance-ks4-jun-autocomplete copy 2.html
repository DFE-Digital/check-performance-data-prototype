    About this guidance

    This is non-statutory guidance from the Department for Education (DfE). It helps state-funded secondary schools, special schools, independent schools, and FE colleges with 14 to 16 provision understand their responsibilities for checking their performance data.

    What we mean in this guidance

    School: any organisation eligible to take part in the KS4 June checking exercise

    Pupil: includes students in FE colleges with 14 to 16 provision

    Exam period: 7 May to 23 June 2026 – see Ofqual guidance for further information

    Spring census day 2026: 15 January 2026 – see census dates guidance for further information

    Checking exercise: the KS4 June checking exercise

    Who this guidance is for

    • school leaders, school staff and governing bodies in all state funded secondary schools (maintained schools, academies, free schools, studio schools and university technical colleges)
    • special schools (maintained schools, academies and free schools)
    • registered independent schools
    • further education colleges with 14 to 16 provision

    What the KS4 performance measures cover in the 2025 to 2026 academic year

    The DfE publishes school performance measures every year. These measures help compare how well schools support pupils' attainment and progress.

    The 2025 to 2026 KS4 performance measures will cover pupils who completed their KS4 studies in that academic year. This may include some qualifications achieved in earlier years, such as 2024 to 2025.

    Progress 8 will not be included for 2025 to 2026. This is because there is no Key Stage 2 (KS2) prior attainment data available from COVID-affected years, which is needed to calculate Progress 8. There will be no replacement measure.

    English Baccalaureate (EBacc) headline and additional measures will also be removed from 2025 to 2026, following our response to the Curriculum and Assessment Review.

    You can find more information about how KS4 performance measures are calculated in the secondary accountability measures guidance.

    What data to check in June and Autumn

    The June checking exercise is the only opportunity to check that we have assigned the correct KS4 pupils for 2025 to 2026 provisional performance measures.

    All school types should take part.

    There will be a separate KS4 Autumn checking exercise in October. This will run for 2 weeks and you can only request to:

    • remove a pupil who was not on your June checking exercise list
    • merge 2 duplicate pupil records

    You will not be able to request any other amendments in the Autumn exercise. You must request all other amendments during the June exercise.

    You cannot ask to remove a pupil in the Autumn exercise if you have already asked for their removal in the June exercise.

    How to check your data

    You can check your data using the check your performance measures data service from Monday 15 June to 5pm on Friday 26 June 2026.

    This is the only chance for you to check that we have assigned the right KS4 pupils to your school to calculate the provisional performance measures.

    We recommend you sign in to the service and check your data as soon as you can, in case you need any additional support.

    Steps you need to take

    Here are the steps you need to take when reviewing your data:

    1. Familiarise yourself with this guidance, including what evidence you need to provide when requesting to remove a pupil.
    2. Sign in to the service and download your school’s CSV file(s) and check we have listed the correct pupils at the end of KS4.
    3. Use the service to request any changes to incorrect data such as add, include or remove a pupil (this is important to ensure the accuracy of the data we publish in October 2026 and revised data published in early 2027).
    4. More than 1 user can request amendments and edit them during the two week checking window.

    What if your school has closed and reopened

    To make sure the right school is held responsible for each year’s performance measures, we assign results to the school as it was at the start of the academic year.

    We treat this date as 12 September, to take account of differing start term dates.

    If a school closes and reopens with the same 7-digit local authority establishment (LAESTAB) number, we usually treat it as the same school.

    If a school closes and reopens with a new establishment number, we treat it as a new school for accountability purposes.

    Remove a pupil that has been added back to the data

    If your school asked to change a pupil from year 11 to year 10 during the 2025 KS4 June checking exercise, we have added that pupil back this year.

    This means the pupil will be included in your 2025 to 2026 performance measures, even if they are no longer on your roll.

    In some cases, you can ask to remove an added-back pupil. See the guidance on add-back removals for more information.

    If you asked to remove a pupil for any other reason in the 2025 June checking exercise and this was approved, that pupil will not be added back this year.

    If you ask to record a pupil aged 15 or over as year 10 in this checking exercise, they will be counted in your performance measures next year.

    How to check your pupil data

    Use the CYPMD service to download your school’s pupil data in CSV format and check it. If there is no data for your school, you will not see files to download.

    You may see up to 2 CSV files:

    • Pupils included in the calculation of your school’s performance measure
    • Pupils non-included, for example pupils who joined your roll after the Spring census day (15 January 2026), or 15-year-olds not in year 11

    If you have no pupils in the non-included group, you will only see the pupils included file.

    How pupil mobility is calculated

    Pupil mobility is only calculated for state funded schools:

    • a mobile pupil is a child who joined the school after the start of year 10
    • a non-mobile pupil is a child who was at the school for all of year 10 and Year 11

    You cannot change admission dates during the checking exercise.

    If a school has a high percentage of mobile pupils with an earlier admission date provided in a previous census, we use that earlier date instead. This means those pupils will no longer be counted as mobile.

    If there is no earlier admission date on record, the pupils will still be counted as mobile when the school’s mobility figures are calculated later in the year.

    Merge pupil records

    All schools can now request to merge an included pupil’s record with another pupil’s record, whether that pupil is included or not, in their performance measures data.

    If we’ve listed duplicate pupil records by mistake, for example because names or dates of birth are different, you can ask to merge them into one record. 

    Evidence you must provide 

    You must provide the following information and evidence, or your request will be rejected:

    • the CYPMD ID of the pupil record you want to delete and merge (this is in the pupil CSV file)
    • further details explaining why the pupil records should be merged 

    Request to include a pupil

    Check your school’s pupil non-included CSV file using the CYPMD service. If you find a pupil who should be included in your performance measures data, you must submit a request to include a pupil using the service.

    You do not have to provide evidence or additional information. You can add optional information or evidence to support your request if you want.

    You cannot ask to include a pupil whose year group has been changed to year 12. This is because a pupil cannot appear twice in published performance measures data.

    Adding an unlisted pupil

    You can request to add a pupil using the CYPMD service during the June checking exercise.

    Important: before you do this, check your school’s pupil CSV files to make sure the pupil is not already listed.

    Only request to add a pupil if they:

    • are at the end of KS4 (year 11)
    • sat exams this year
    • are a pupil you want your school to be accountable for in this year’s published performance measures

    Do not request to add a pupil who does not meet these criteria.

    Information you must provide

    It is important you provide enough information about the pupil. If you do not, we cannot add them to your school. You must provide:

    • First name
    • Last name
    • Date of birth
    • Sex
    • Unique Pupil Number (UPN)
    • Admission date
    • Special educational needs (SEN) status
    • Year group
    • An explanation of why the pupil should be included in your school’s performance measures data

    Additional evidence you can provide if available

    • education history before arriving in England
    • education history since arriving – names and dates of schools
    • immigration or resettlement documents – for example, Afghan Citizens Resettlement Scheme
    • any other supporting information

    How to remove a pupil from your data

    For each pupil removal category, you must provide specific information or evidence to support your request.

    We review these requirements every year to make sure they are:

    • appropriate for each category
    • reasonable for schools to provide

    For 2026, we have published some new guidance which focuses on categories:

    • with low acceptance rates
    • where schools need clearer guidance on evidence

    If you want to request to remove a pupil, we recommend that you read KS4 June Pupil Removal Requests: Tips for schools and colleges before submitting any requests.

    Make sure you submit the correct information and evidence

    You must submit all requests and supporting information or evidence by 5pm on Friday 26 June 2026.

    To improve the chances of your request being accepted, follow the best practice examples below when providing information or evidence, if applicable.

    ✔ Do

    • upload your information or evidence in PDF format only
    • use the free text box in the service, when available, to briefly explain the pupil’s situation
    • ensure your evidence supports what you have written in the free text box
    • make sure your information or evidence is clear, concise and relevant to the removal category
    • check that any evidence meets the required dates for that category, if applicable
    • include supporting documents from external sources, where appropriate, for example, social workers, local authorities, medical professionals and the police

    ✘ Do not

    • request a pupil amendment unless the pupil’s circumstances meet the criteria for an amendment category, this helps avoid unnecessary work
    • provide more than 6 pages of information or evidence, we will not read anything over this limit
    • compare decisions about one pupil amendment request with another, as each request is considered individually

    How we make decisions

    We have a comprehensive moderation process in place to make sure all decisions are fair and consistent.

    We review:

    • each pupil amendment request
    • the supporting information or evidence (up to 6 pages)

    We may also carry out internal data checks as part of this process.

    The DfE decision is final. We cannot give individual feedback on decisions due to the high number of requests we receive.

    When requests to remove a pupil will not be accepted

    Do not make a pupil removal request if the:

    • pupil had a short-term illness (for example, a virus and was absent from school for up to a month)
    • pupil had a short-term illness and missed some or all exams
    • pupil is still on roll and receiving home tuition or remote learning
    • request is based solely on the pupil’s special educational needs and disabilities (SEND)
    • school was affected by RAAC and now has arrangements so pupils can access education and sit exams
    • pupil is a non-attender (includes any absence unless for reasons of long term illness)
    • pupil is from a traveller family
    • pupil has left to re-sit exams at an FE college (except those with 14 to 16 provision)
    • pupil attends an FE college full or part time, unless their main registration is at an FE college with 14 to 16 provision

    Evidence required for removing a pupil

    You can ask to remove certain pupils from your school’s performance measures if they meet specific criteria.

    Admitted following permanent exclusion

    If you admit a pupil who was permanently excluded from another state-funded school after 1 September 2024, you can request for them to be removed from your school’s performance measures.

    Read the KS4 June pupil removal requests guidance for tips and examples.

    Important: do not submit a request for a pupil who was permanently excluded before 1 September 2024, as it will be rejected.

    Evidence you must provide

    You must provide all the following information and evidence, or your request will be rejected:

    • DfE number of the excluding school
    • date of exclusion (must be after 1 September 2024)
    • evidence to support your request, such as a letter from the headteacher notifying the parent or guardian of the permanent exclusion

    Admitted from abroad with English not first language

    You can request to remove a pupil from your school’s performance measures if:

    • English is not their first language
    • English is not an official language of their country of origin
    • they first joined a school in England on or after 1 September 2023

    Your request will be rejected if the pupil’s country of origin has English as an official language, or if the 2026 spring census (15 January 2026) records English as their first language.

    Check the countries list to see if your request is likely to be accepted or rejected (if all other conditions are met).

    Read the KS4 June pupil removal requests guidance for tips and examples.

    Important: we may still consider your request in exceptional cases if the pupil arrived from abroad but first joined a school in England before 1 September 2023, or has attended more than one school in England.

    Evidence you must provide

    You must provide all the following information and evidence, or your request will be rejected:

    • pupil’s first language
    • pupil’s country of origin
    • pupil’s date of admission to your school
    • date they first joined a school in England
    • date they arrived in England

    If you want us to consider exceptional circumstances, you must also provide the following additional evidence, where applicable:

    • brief details of the pupil’s education before they arrived in England, including school names and dates (if known)
    • details of their education in England, including school names and dates (if known)
    • any relevant documents from an external organisation, such as asylum, immigration or resettlement schemes (for example, the Afghan Citizens Resettlement Scheme)
    • any other information or evidence to support your request

    Child missing education

    A child missing education is a child of compulsory school age who is not registered at a school and is not receiving suitable education otherwise than at a school.

    A child reaches compulsory school age on or after their fifth birthday.

    If they turn 5 between:

    • 1 January and 31 March, they reach school age on 31 March
    • 1 April and 31 August, they reach school age on 31 August
    • 1 September and 31 December, they reach school age on 31 December

    A child continues to be of compulsory school age until the last Friday of June in the school year they turn 16.

    Important: if a pupil is registered at a school but is often or severely absent, they are not classed as a child missing education. This is non-attendance and is not a reason for removal.

    A pupil’s name can only be deleted for a reason set out in Regulation 9 of the School Attendance (Pupil Registration) (England) Regulations 2024.

    Deleting a pupil’s name for any other reason is unlawful. There are no set timescales, as each case is different.

    You can request to remove a pupil from your school’s performance measures if they:

    • did not return after a leave of absence, and the school and local authority have checked but cannot find them, or agree they are unlikely to return, and the pupil was removed from the school roll under Ground H of the school attendance regulations after the 2026 Spring census
    • has been continually absent for 20 school days, and the school and local authority have checked but cannot find them, or agree they are unlikely to return, and the pupil was removed from the school roll under Ground I of the school attendance regulations

    Evidence you must provide

    You must provide all the following information and evidence, or your request will be rejected:

    • the date the pupil was removed from your school roll
    • evidence from the school and local authority showing they worked together to try to find the pupil and confirm their circumstances, including the date the local authority was told, as outlined in the children missing education guidance for schools and local authorities
    • this could include a completed joint school and local authority CME checklist

    Child has died

    If a pupil has sadly died, please provide the date you removed them from your school roll.

    Evidence you must provide

    You must provide the following information or your request will be rejected:

    • the date the pupil was removed from your school roll

    Dual registered or moved school

    Dual registration means a pupil is registered at more than one setting for some or all of their education.

    You can also ask to remove a pupil if they have left your school to attend another school.

    If a dual-registered pupil appears in your pupil included CSV file, and you think the other school should be responsible, you can ask to request them from your school’s performance measures.

    Read the KS4 June pupil removal requests guidance for tips and examples.

    In some cases, the local authority may have asked you to add a pupil to your roll to release funding for a place at another setting, such as a specialist or alternative provision.

    If the pupil has never attended your school, you must provide evidence from the local authority to confirm this.

    Before you submit a request, check these rules for dual registered pupils:

    • if a pupil is dual registered at two state-funded schools, their results are counted at the main school
    • a dual-registered pupil is only included in performance measures once, as shown in the table below
    Mainstream School Alternative Provision (AP) Non-Hospital Special School Hospital Special School Assign pupil to Reason
    Yes✓ Yes✓ Yes✓ Yes✓ Mainstream school AP performance measures are not published
    Yes✓ Yes✓ Yes✓ Yes✓ School of main registration Both schools’ performance measures are published
    Yes✓ Yes✓ Yes✓ Yes✓ Mainstream school AP performance measures are not published
    Yes✓ Yes✓ Yes✓ Yes✓ Non-hospital special school AP performance measures are not published
    Yes✓ Yes✓ Yes✓ Yes✓ Non-hospital special school Hospital school performance measures are not published

    Important: Before you submit a request for a dual-registered or moved pupil:

    • work with the other school, as they must submit an ‘add or include pupil’ request during the June checking exercise, requests made after this will be rejected
    • the school you ask to transfer accountability to must be published at KS4, requests for pupils who moved to a non-published KS4 school will be rejected
    • we will reject your request if the pupil is not added or included at another school during the June checking exercise

    Alternative provision (AP) settings, such as pupil referral units, AP academies and some independent schools, do not have performance measures published.

    A pupil’s results will instead count towards the mainstream or non-hospital special school they were on roll at the time of the 2026 Spring census on 15 January 2026.

    Evidence you must provide

    You must provide the following information or your request will be rejected:

    • the DfE number of the school where accountability for the pupil should be transferred to

    Elective home education

    If a pupil left your school for elective home education before the Spring census day on 15 January 2026, you can ask for them to be removed from your school’s performance measures. 

    Read the KS4 June pupil removal requests guidance for tips and examples.

    Important: If the pupil left your school for elective home education after the Spring census day on 15 January 2026, your request will be rejected. 

    Evidence you must provide 

    You must provide all the following information and evidence, or your request will be rejected:

    • the date the pupil was removed from your school roll
    • a copy of the notification your school sent to the local authority when the pupil was removed
    • if applicable, an explanation of why the pupil was still on roll on the Spring census day on 15 January 2026 

    Not on roll at the time of January census

    For independent schools and FE colleges with 14 to 16 provision, we allocate pupils to a school or college using exam data from awarding organisations and information from the Individualised Learner Record (ILR).

    If we have incorrectly included pupils who are not on your roll, you can request to remove them from your school or college’s performance measures.

    Evidence you must provide

    You must provide the following evidence or your request will be rejected:

    • evidence that the pupil was not on roll, for example if they were a private candidate

    Permanently excluded from current school

    If a pupil was permanently excluded before the Spring census day on 15 January 2026, you can request to remove them from your school’s performance measures.

    Important: if a pupil was permanently excluded after the Spring census day on 15 January 2026, we will reject your request to remove them from your performance measures.

    You cannot use suspensions as a reason to remove a pupil from your school’s performance measures. In the legislation, a suspension is described as a fixed-term exclusion.

    Evidence you must provide

    You must provide the following evidence or your request will be rejected:

    • proof of permanent exclusion
    • date of permanent exclusion
    • DfE number of the school the pupil went on to (if this is not known please use ‘0000000’ for the DfE number when requesting the pupil removal within the checking exercise portal)
    • explanation detailing why the pupil was recorded on 2026 spring census day (15 January 2026), and, if applicable
    • evidence of exclusion review outcome

    Permanently left England

    If a pupil has permanently left England and was removed from your school roll before the exam period, you can ask for them to be removed from your performance measures.

    Read the KS4 June pupil removal requests guidance for tips and examples.

    Important: you cannot request to remove a pupil if they permanently left England and were removed from your school roll on or after 7 May 2026 (the start of the exam period).

    Evidence you must provide

    You must provide all the following information and evidence, or your request will be rejected:

    • the country the pupil moved to
    • the date they were taken off your roll (this must be before 7 May 2026)
    • a copy of the notification you sent to the local authority confirming the pupil was removed from your roll

    Social care involvement – including police or prison

    You can ask us to remove a pupil from your school’s performance measures if their circumstances meet one or more of the situations below and you provide the required evidence.

    Read the KS4 June pupil removal requests guidance for tips and examples.

    Social care involvement

    You can request to remove a pupil if they have been involved in a recent or ongoing situation requiring social care support, and this has seriously affected their ability to access education (in person or online) or sit all their exams.

    Reports from your school’s internal systems (for example, CPOMS) do not count as third party external evidence.

    Evidence from social services or another relevant external agency must be dated in 2026 and before exams start. It must clearly show the pupil’s circumstances and how social services involvement affected their education (in school or online).

    Evidence you must provide

    You must provide all the following information and evidence, or your request will be rejected:

    • external third party evidence from social services that shows how the situation seriously affected the pupil’s ability to access education (in person or online) or sit all their exams
    • the evidence must be dated in 2026 before exams started on 7 May 2026
    • this could include a social worker’s report or multi-agency meeting minutes
    Important: you must provide third party external evidence. A school or college’s narrative on its own will be rejected.

    Police involvement

    You can request to remove a pupil if they have been involved in a recent police situation and any restrictions have affected their ability to access education (in person or online), or to sit all their exams.

    Evidence you must provide

    You must provide the following information and evidence, or your request will be rejected:

    • a police report that shows what restrictions were put in place and how they prevented the pupil from accessing education (in person or online) or sitting all their exams
    • any other evidence to support your request, for example how the situation affected the pupil, if applicable
    Important: you must provide a police report or your request will be rejected.

    Prison, remand or secure unit

    You can request to remove a pupil if they have been detained in a prison, remand centre or secure unit for at least 4 months.

    If the pupil was sectioned under the Mental Health Act, you must use the ‘life-limiting or critical illness’ category when you submit your request.

    Evidence you must provide

    You must provide the following information and evidence, or your request will be rejected:

    • external evidence from a prison, remand centre, secure unit, the Ministry of Justice or the police showing the dates the pupil was detained (these must total at least 4 months)
    • if applicable, any other evidence to support your request, including details of restrictions put in place that prevented the pupil from accessing education (in person or online)

    Life-limiting or critical illness

    You must provide third party external medical evidence from a health professional.

    A school or college’s own account is not enough.

    We will reject requests that are only based on a pupil’s SEND status.

    The evidence must meet the date requirements. If it does not, we will reject your request.

    Your evidence must clearly show how the illness or injury affected the pupil and their ability to access education (in person or online).

    Read the KS4 June pupil removal requests guidance for tips and examples.

    Life-limiting illness

    You can request to remove a pupil from your performance measures if they have a life-limiting illness.

    Evidence you must provide

    You must provide the following information and evidence, or your request will be rejected:

    • evidence from a medical professional confirming that the illness is life-limiting

    Critical illness

    You can ask us to remove a pupil from your school’s performance measures if they have a critical illness or condition that:

    • has lasted, or is being investigated, for at least 12 months before KS4 exams start on 7 May 2026
    • has seriously affected their ability to access education or sit all their exams

    This could include needing an organ transplant or a severe and ongoing mental illness, such as psychosis or a condition requiring long-term in-patient care.

    Evidence you must provide

    You must provide the following information and evidence, or your request will be rejected:

    • evidence from a medical professional, dated in 2026 and before exams start on 7 May 2026, showing that the pupil has been unable to access education (in person or online) for prolonged periods or sit all their exams
    • evidence from a medical professional confirming the pupil has had, or been under investigation for, a critical illness or condition for at least 12 months before KS4 exams start on 7 May 2026

    In some cases, one piece of medical evidence may be enough if it includes all the information needed for points 1 and 2.

    Recent life changing illness or injury

    You can ask us to remove a pupil if they have a recent diagnosis of a life-changing illness or injury that has seriously affected their ability to access education (in person or online) or sit all their exams.

    For example, this could include a cancer diagnosis or a limb amputation.

    Evidence you must provide

    You must provide the following evidence or your request will be rejected:

    • evidence from a medical professional, dated in 2026 and before exams start on 7 May 2026, showing that the pupil has been unable to access education (in person or online) or sit all their exams

    Pupil is pregnant or given birth

    If a pupil is pregnant or has recently given birth, you must provide confirmation, for example a MAT B1 maternity certificate.

    You must also provide a statement explaining how the pregnancy or birth affected the pupil’s ability to access education (in person or online) or sit all their exams.

    Change a pupil’s year group

    Important

    Year 11 is the end of KS4. If you request to change a pupil’s year group to anything other than Year 11, they will no longer count as being at the end of KS4. This means they will be left out of your school’s 2025 to 2026 performance measures.

    If you record a pupil aged 15 or over as being in Year 10, they will not count as being at the end of KS4 for your school’s 2025 to 2026 performance measures. However, they will be reported against your school’s results the following year under the ‘add-back’ rule.

    If you request to change a pupil from Year 11 to Year 12 or above, we will check the records where they were previously counted as being at the end of KS4. This is because pupils cannot skip Year 11.

    Evidence you must provide

    If the year group changes to below Year 11, you must provide the following information and evidence, or your request will be rejected:

    • the year group the pupil should be moved to
    • an explanation of why the year group needs to be changed

    If the year group changes to above Year 11, you must provide the following information and evidence, or your request will be rejected:

    • the year group the pupil should be moved to
    • the DfE establishment number of the school where the pupil was last recorded at the end of KS4
    • any other evidence to support your request

    Remove a pupil who has been added back

    If a pupil has been added back to your school (with inclusion status code 403), you can request for them to be removed from your performance measures due to the following circumstances:

    • Admitted from abroad with English not first language
    • Pupil has died
    • Permanently left England

    If a pupil has been added back to your school for the ‘admitted from abroad with English not first language’ reason, their first admission to a school in England can be on or after 1 September 2022.

    Completed KS4 studies this academic year in Year 11 at another school

    If a pupil left your school and finished year 11 at another school, you can ask for them to be removed from your performance measures.

    Evidence you must provide

    You must provide the following information or your request will be rejected:

    • the DfE number of the school where accountability for the pupil should be transferred to
    Important: the school you request to move the pupil to must have published KS4 results. You cannot request this if the pupil moved to a school without published KS4 results.

    Alternative provision (AP) settings, such as pupil referral units, AP academies and some independent schools, do not have published performance measures.

    This means the pupil’s results will count towards the last mainstream or non-hospital special school they were on roll at the Spring census on 15 January 2026.

    Other

    From 2026, you can use a new option to request for a pupil added back to your school this year to be removed.

    Only use this option if:

    • the pupil’s situation is not covered by the options above
    • your school did not ask to change their year group during the June 2025 KS4 checking exercise

    We will check our data to make sure this is correct before we decide on your request.

    Evidence you must provide

    You must provide the following information and evidence, or your request will be rejected:

    • an explanation why you want to remove the pupil who has been added back to your school
    • evidence to support your request, such as a permanent exclusion letter sent to the parent or carer, or a notification to the local authority confirming the pupil was removed from your roll
